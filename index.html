Grammar I

Present Progressive: am / are / is + verb-ing

We use the present progressive to talk about what people are wearing right now.

Demonstrative Pronouns: that and those

Solved Examples (Basic, Intermediate, Advanced)

Basic Level

Basic - Example 1

Question: What are you wearing? I'm wearing a ______ (skirt / hat).

Solution: skirt. Complete sentence: "I'm wearing a skirt."
Basic - Example 2

Question: What are you wearing? I'm wearing ______ jacket.

Solution: a. Complete sentence: "I'm wearing a jacket."
Basic - Example 3

Question: What are you wearing? I'm wearing a T-______.

Solution: shirt. Complete sentence: "I'm wearing a T-shirt."
Basic - Example 4

Question: What is that? ______ my pink hat.

Solution: That's. Complete sentence: "That's my pink hat."
Basic - Example 5

Question: What are those? Those ______ my brown pants.

Solution: are. Complete sentence: "Those are my brown pants."

Intermediate Level

Intermediate - Example 6

Question: What is your brother wearing? My brother ______ wearing pants.

Solution: is. Complete sentence: "My brother is wearing pants."
Intermediate - Example 7

Question: Form the question for a dress: What ______ that?

Solution: 's (What's that?). Answer: "That's my dress."
Intermediate - Example 8

Question: Form the question for socks: What ______ those?

Solution: are (What are those?). Answer: "Those are my socks."
Intermediate - Example 9

Question: Fill in pronoun and verb: ______ wearing a red shirt (referring to a girl).

Solution: She's (She is). Complete sentence: "She's wearing a red shirt."
Intermediate - Example 10

Question: Fill in pronoun and verb: ______ wearing blue shoes (referring to a boy).

Solution: He's (He is). Complete sentence: "He's wearing blue shoes."

Advanced Level

Advanced - Example 11

Question: Complete dialogue:
— What ______ your sister wearing?
— ______ wearing a yellow dress and white shoes.

Solution: is / She's. Full dialogue: "What is your sister wearing? She's wearing a yellow dress and white shoes."
Advanced - Example 12

Question: Correct the error: "What is those? That are my gloves."

Solution: "What are those? Those are my gloves." (Match plural demonstrative 'those' with 'are').
Advanced - Example 13

Question: Expand contractions: "What's that? That's my shirt."

Solution: "What is that? That is my shirt."
Advanced - Example 14

Question: Pointing at a distant picture of your brother wearing a jacket. Ask and answer using 'that'.

Solution: "What's that? That's my brother wearing a jacket."
Advanced - Example 15

Question: Complete complex sentence: "My mother ______ (wear) a green skirt and my father ______ (wear) brown pants."

Solution: is wearing / is wearing. Full sentence: "My mother is wearing a green skirt and my father is wearing brown pants."

Critical Reflection Questions

Reflection 1

Question: Why do we use "is" with "your brother" or "the girl", but "are" with "you"?

Answer: Subject-verb agreement rules. "You" takes the verb are, while third-person singular subjects take is.
Reflection 2

Question: What is the difference between pointing at a single hat far away ("that") versus multiple socks far away ("those")?

Answer: "That" is singular, whereas "those" is plural. The verb changes from is to are accordingly.
Reflection 3

Question: How does adding "-ing" to action verbs (like wear -> wearing) help us describe live actions happening right now?

Answer: The suffix "-ing" turns an action into an ongoing process happening at the current moment.
